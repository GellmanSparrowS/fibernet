# Quasistatic member removal

A synthetic notched, triangulated frame with 63 nodes and 158 circular Timoshenko beams. The animation displays every recorded observation of one completed FiberNet FractureSolver run, in order. It shows actual removal masks on the undeformed reference geometry, synchronized with computed reaction and elastic strain energy versus imposed top-row displacement. Red dashed segments mark members newly removed since the preceding observation, with their stable IDs.

The observed sequence removes 17 members, in addition to the six-member initial notch, and terminates at material-path loss at 0.1068936536 mm. Removal occurs near the loaded boundary rather than advancing the notch. This outcome is retained. The terminating zero reaction/energy values are the recorded disconnected-path result, not evidence of recoil, fragment motion, or fracture-energy dissipation.

## Model and interpretation

- Welded linear-static Timoshenko frame; bottom fixed and top prescribed in +y with other top DOFs constrained.
- Synthetic material and strengths; no experimental calibration or fracture toughness.
- The geometry is not deformed in this visualization. No dynamic crack, contact, recoil, fragment trajectory, or finite-deformation result is implied.
- Frame order is observation order, not physical time. Avalanches keep their actual repeated imposed displacements. Durations are presentation holds only; no interpolated solver states are added.
- All result scope flags, solver diagnostics, event records, completion/termination metadata, and original stable IDs are retained in replay.json.

## Files and reproduction

- `fracture_sequence.gif`: 960 × 1358 (A4 portrait aspect), 31 recorded observations.
- `fracture_poster.svg`: editable vector rendering of the final observation, without external resources.
- `replay.json`: compact saved geometry, complete result, scenario, run summary and source hashes.
- `render.py`: relative-path renderer; run `python render.py` with Pillow and DejaVu Sans fonts installed. It imports no FiberNet solver and does not rerun physics.
- `keyframe_*.png`: generated by the renderer from decoded GIF observations 0, 2, 16 and 30; not supplied in this bundle.
